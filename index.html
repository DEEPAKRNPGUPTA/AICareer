Curriculum that builds on itself

Each stage connects the foundation to the next stage, ending with a complete AI-driven business workflow.

Weeks 1–2
Python & Data
CSV, Excel, pandas basics, aggregations, charts, real datasets and a practical introduction to Python.

Deliverable: data reports and reusable Python scripts across four domains.

Weeks 3–4
Machine Learning
Features, training, validation, classification, regression and business evaluation.

Deliverable: simple predictive models with clear business explanations.

Weeks 5–7
LLMs & RAG
Prompting, embeddings, retrieval, document-grounded answers and AI application integration.

Deliverable: an evidence-based automobile service assistant.

Weeks 8–10
Agentic Workflows
Tool calling, orchestration, agent roles, approvals, exception handling and deployment.

Deliverable: a controlled service and inventory agentic workflow.

Real data, real questions

Projects across four domains

We begin with accessible examples in every domain. In later LLM and agentic stages, the automobile service and inventory scenario becomes the main integrated project.

🛒

Retail

Summarize daily sales, identify inventory gaps, predict demand and explore price and promotion decisions.

🚗

Automobile

Study work-floor parts usage, predict inventory needs, support service questions and orchestrate replenishment approvals.

🏥

Healthcare

Explore appointment demand, resource usage, patient-support analytics and basic prediction examples.

💻

Software Asset Management

Analyze licenses, software usage, renewals and opportunities to optimize spend.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Do I need prior AI or ML experience?

No prior AI or ML project experience is required. The journey begins with Python and real data; basic engineering and programming familiarity will help.

How long is the course?

The planned journey is 10 weeks, with flexibility based on the batch and learning pace.

How much is the course fee?

The fee is intended to be minimal. Contact Deepak Gupta for current fee and batch details.

Will I build practical projects?

Yes. The program emphasizes hands-on exercises and project work across retail, healthcare, automobile and software asset management.

Is this affiliated with an automobile manufacturer?

No. The automobile scenario is a generic learning example, not a manufacturer-sponsored program.
